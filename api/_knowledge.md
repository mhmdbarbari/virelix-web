## Process

1. Discover: We ask about your business, your users and your competitors before any design work starts.
2. Plan: A clear map of the site structure, content and how it should feel to move through.
3. Design: Typography, color, components and interactions, put together as one system, not one-off screens.
4. Develop: Production code, tested and checked against real performance numbers, not just “looks fine”.
5. Launch: Deployment, analytics and tracking set up before launch day, not scrambled together after.
6. Grow: We keep iterating and running campaigns after launch. The site isn’t “done”, it’s maintained.
